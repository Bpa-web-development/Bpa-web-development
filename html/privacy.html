Privacy Policy and Cookies

What we collect, why we collect it, and how to see or delete it. Effective October 25, 2026.

What we collect How we use it Cookies Who we share with Your choices Contact

The short version

  • We only collect what we need to run your account, your orders and the help desk.
  • Your password is never stored. We keep only a one-way bcrypt hash of it.
  • We never collect payment card details, and nothing is charged.
  • We use no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and we never sell or share your information for advertising.
  • You can delete your account yourself at any time, or email us and we'll delete everything.

Who we are

Marlowby is a fictional subscription box company created by a student team for the Business Professionals of America (BPA) 365 Website Design and Development Team event. This website is run by that team, which is responsible for the information collected here. You can reach us at marlowbybox@gmail.com.

What we collect

We collect only what you type into our forms, plus a small amount of technical information needed to keep the site secure.

Information collected by each part of the site
When youWe collect
Create an accountYour name, email address, and a bcrypt hash of your password (never the password itself). Later, the ZIP code you ship to.
Place an orderYour name, email, ZIP code, the plans in your cart, any promo code and any gift note. We do not collect card or bank details.
Send a help desk ticketYour name, email, topic, message and an optional order number.
Join the newsletterYour email address and the topics you pick.
Visit any pageYour IP address, used for a few hours to stop abuse such as repeated password guessing. We store it only as a one-way hash. Our hosting provider also keeps standard server logs for a short time.

How we use it

  • To create your account, keep you signed in and show your orders.
  • To send emails you ask for or expect: a welcome email, order confirmations, help desk replies and, if you subscribe, the newsletter.
  • To let our team see new orders, accounts and help desk tickets so we can answer you.
  • To protect the site, for example by limiting repeated sign-in attempts.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or automated decisions, and we never sell it.

Cookies and browser storage

Marlowby uses one cookie and some of your browser's local storage. All of it is strictly necessary for features you use, so it is on by default. We do not use advertising, analytics or third-party tracking cookies.

Cookies and storage used by Marlowby

mb_sessionCookie (essential)Keeps you signed in. It holds a random code, never your password, and scripts on the page cannot read it.30 days, or until you sign out

Local storage stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can clear cookies and site data at any time in your browser's settings. If you block the mb_session cookie, you won't be able to stay signed in.

Fonts: our pages load the Lato and Playfair Display typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when a page loads. Google Fonts does not set cookies. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the services that run the site for us, and only so they can do that job:

  • Vercel hosts the website and its server code.
  • Neon hosts our database.
  • Google delivers our email through Gmail and serves our fonts.

We may also share information if the law requires it. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined in the California Consumer Privacy Act.

How long we keep it

  • Account: until you delete it. Deleting your account removes your profile and every saved sign-in right away.
  • Orders and help desk tickets: 12 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe, then deleted within 30 days.
  • Abuse protection records: a few hours.

When the BPA project ends, we will delete all of the information above.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account any time from the account menu after you sign in.
  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter with the link in any newsletter email or the button in the footer.
  • See, correct or delete anything we hold about you, including orders and tickets, by emailing marlowbybox@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days and won't treat you differently for asking.

Depending on where you live, such as California or the European Union, you may have additional rights under laws like the CCPA or the GDPR. Where those laws apply, we rely on what's needed to give you the service you asked for, and on your consent for the newsletter, which you can withdraw at any time. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

How we protect it

The site uses HTTPS everywhere. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt, sign-in cookies are HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite, sign-in attempts are rate limited, and every form is checked again on our server. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else. If we ever learn of a breach that affects you, we will email you.

Children

Marlowby is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects how we use information we already have, we'll email account holders first.

Contact us

Questions or requests about your privacy: marlowbybox@gmail.com, or use the help desk.
