          Questions about plans

          When will I be charged?

          Monthly plans are charged on the 1st of each month, and boxes ship on the 5th. Six-month and yearly plans are charged once, on the day you subscribe.

          Can I skip a month or cancel?

          Yes. Skip or cancel from your account before the 1st and you won't be charged for that month's box.

          How do you choose each month's city?

          We rotate through regions so every box feels new, and we look for cities with a strong community of small, independent makers. You can see earlier boxes on our Past Months page.

          Can I change plans later?

          Yes. Upgrades and downgrades start with your next box. Switching from a yearly plan to monthly takes effect at your renewal date.
