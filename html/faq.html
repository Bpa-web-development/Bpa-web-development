Questions and answers

01

Boxes and shipping

When does my box ship?

Boxes ship on the 5th with tracking and arrive in 3 to 6 business days.

Which city is in next month's box?

It's a secret until it ships. This month is Burlington, VT. Watch your email and the plans page for hints.

What's inside a box?

Basic holds 4 to 5 local goods, Premium 7 to 9. Every box adds a maker card and a passport stamp.

How much is shipping?

Free on every plan and gift, anywhere in the U.S., including Alaska and Hawaii.

Something arrived broken. What do I do?

Snap a photo and open a ticket within 14 days. We'll replace or refund it.

02

Plans and billing

How much do the plans cost?

Basic is $34 and Premium $59 a month. Pay for 6 months ($31, $54) or a year ($29, $50) to save. Compare plans.

Can I skip a month or cancel?

Yes, anytime. Change before the 1st and that month isn't charged. After the 1st, it starts next month.

When will I be charged?

At checkout, then on the 1st when your plan renews.

Can I switch between Basic and Premium?

Yes. Changes before the 1st apply that month, and upgrades only charge the difference.

03

Gifts

How do gift boxes work?

Send 3, 6 or 12 prepaid boxes ($99, $189, $349) with a note. Gifts never renew.

Does the person I'm gifting get a passport?

Yes, printed with their name. Every box adds a new stamp.

04

Deals and codes

Where do I enter a promo code?

In your cart, or tap Apply in the code wallet. One code per order.

How do friend codes work?

Your first name plus -MB10, like JORDAN-MB10. Friends get $10 off their first order.

How do I get the free tote bag?

Spend $300 and it's added free, shipped in your first box.

05

Your account

Is my account information safe?

Your account is stored on our secure server. Your password is saved only as a one-way bcrypt hash, so nobody, including us, can read it.

What are passport stamps?

A keepsake that gets a new city stamp with every box you open.
