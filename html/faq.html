Questions and answers

01

Boxes and shipping

When does my box ship?

Every box leaves our Burlington warehouse on the 5th of the month. You'll get an email with tracking that day, and most boxes arrive in 3 to 6 business days.

Which city is in next month's box?

That's the fun part: every future box is a mystery. We only reveal a city when its box ships. This month's city is Burlington, VT. Until a box ships, all you get are little hints in your email and on the plans page, so the surprise stays yours.

What's inside a box?

Basic boxes hold 4 to 5 local products and Premium boxes hold 7 to 9, always from small businesses in that month's city. Every box also has a maker stories card and a new stamp for your Marlowby passport.

How much is shipping?

Shipping is free on every plan and every gift to any U.S. address, including Alaska and Hawaii. We don't ship outside the U.S. yet.

Something arrived broken. What do I do?

We're sorry! Snap a photo and open a help desk ticket with your order number within 14 days. We'll send a replacement or a refund for that item, whichever you prefer.

02

Plans and billing

How much do the plans cost?

Basic is $34 a box and Premium is $59 a box when you pay monthly. Paying every 6 months drops that to $31 and $54, and paying yearly drops it to $29 and $50. Compare the plans to see everything side by side.

Can I skip a month or cancel?

Yes, anytime. Skip or cancel before the 1st of the month and you won't be charged for that month's box. After the 1st, that box is already being packed, so the change starts the following month.

When will I be charged?

Your first charge happens at checkout. After that, monthly plans renew on the 1st, and 6-month or yearly plans renew on the 1st of the month your term ends.

Can I switch between Basic and Premium?

Of course. Changes made before the 1st apply to that month's box. Upgrading mid-term only charges the difference for the boxes left in your term.

03

Gifts

How do gift boxes work?

Choose 3, 6 or 12 prepaid boxes for $99, $189 or $349. Gifts never renew, so nobody gets a surprise bill. You pick the start month and add a personal note at checkout.

Does the person I'm gifting get a passport?

Yes. Every gift comes with a Marlowby passport printed with their name, and each box adds that city's stamp. They'll never know which city is coming next either.

04

Deals and codes

Where do I enter a promo code?

Type it in the promo box in your cart, or tap Apply on any code in the code wallet on the Specials page. You can use one code per order, and the savings show before you pay.

How do friend codes work?

Make your own on the Specials page. It's your first name plus -MB10, like JORDAN-MB10. Your friend gets $10 off their first order when they use it.

How do I get the free tote bag?

Once your cart reaches $300 after any discount, a free Burlington tote is added to your order. It ships inside your first box.

05

Your account

Is my account information safe?

In this demo, your account is encrypted and saved only on this device. Your password is never stored. It's used to unlock your encrypted profile, so not even we can read it.

What are passport stamps?

Your Marlowby passport is a keepsake that grows with you. Every box adds an inked stamp for its city, and members get a special stamp when they join. Fill it up and you'll have a record of every city you've explored.
