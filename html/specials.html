Our promise to you

You come first, before any deal

Deals are nice, but how you feel when the box lands on your porch matters more. These four promises come with every Marlowby order, coupon or not.

  • Free shipping, always

    Every box ships free to every state. No minimum, no code needed, no surprise at checkout.

  • Skip or cancel anytime

    Going on vacation? Skip a month. Need a break? Cancel in two taps. No calls, no fees, no guilt.

  • Real people, real help

    Questions about a box, a code or a gift? Our help desk is run by people, not bots. Ask the help desk

  • The happy box promise

    If something arrives broken or just isn't right, tell us and we will make it right with a replacement or a refund.

Deals that give back

Every deal still pays a local maker in full

When you use a code, Marlowby covers the discount, not the small business that made your goods. You save, and the maker still gets their full price.

  • Makers are paid fairly. We agree on a fair price with each maker before anything goes in a box, and a coupon never changes it.
  • Money stays in the city. Your box is bought from shops, farms and studios in that month's city, so the money you spend helps their neighborhood.
  • A new spotlight every month. Each box puts new small makers in front of curious people all across the country. This month it's Burlington.
